## 1. Hypothesis

> In advanced NSCLC receiving first-line systemic therapy, STK11 loss-of-function is associated with shorter overall survival to a similar degree in immune-checkpoint-inhibitor-containing regimens and in platinum-doublet regimens without an ICI, i.e. the treatment-by-STK11 interaction on overall survival is null and the marker is prognostic rather than predictive.

**Origin:** contradiction — This is the central declared direct contradiction in the graph (C-003 vs C-013) and the one the whole field turns on. The resistance claim rests almost entirely on single-arm, ICI-only cohorts (C-003, C-039, C-040) which cannot separate prognosis from prediction. Every design in the graph that carries a non-ICI control arm points the other way: Papillon-Cavanagh's interaction HR is 1.05 (p=0.785) for PFS and 1.13 (p=0.540) for OS (C-012, C-013); Shire reports HR 1.4 for STK11 on first-line chemotherapy with no ICI (C-037) and the same point estimate, nonsignificant, on first-line ICI (C-038); KEYNOTE-042 randomization shows a numerically LARGER pembrolizumab benefit in STK11-mutants (C-018). I expect the data to favour C-012/C-013/C-037 over C-003/C-039. The mapper found no verified post-2022 cohort with n>500 reporting a formal treatment-by-STK11 interaction test, and my own searches did not find one either; that exact design is the gap. A large clinicogenomic cohort with both ICI-treated and chemotherapy-only first-line patients is uniquely able to supply it.

**Supporting claims:** C-003, C-011, C-012, C-013, C-037, C-038, C-039, C-049

### Mechanistic basis (recorded before any outcome was seen)

LKB1 loss removes AMPK-dependent restraint on mTORC1 and on anabolic one-carbon flux, raising proliferative capacity, lineage plasticity and metastatic competence; co-selected KEAP1/NRF2 activation adds broad cytoprotection against oxidative and cytotoxic stress. The survival deficit is therefore generated by tumour growth biology and is realised on platinum doublet as much as on PD-(L)1 blockade. The immune-evasion arm (STING silencing C-029/C-030, immunoproteasome failure C-031) is real but on this account is a minor contributor to total hazard. The chain predicts a non-null STK11 main effect in BOTH strata and a null interaction; it is falsified if the STK11 hazard is materially larger in the ICI stratum. That contrast is the estimand, so the hypothesis tests the mechanism rather than restating the association.

Steps:

1. STK11 loss-of-function abolishes LKB1 kinase activity and hence AMPK activation under energy stress.
2. Loss of AMPK restraint de-represses mTORC1 and anabolic serine/one-carbon flux, supporting growth under nutrient and oxidative stress.
3. The resulting phenotype raises the hazard of death independently of which systemic agent is given.
4. Co-selected KEAP1/NRF2 activation blunts platinum cytotoxicity and oxidative effector mechanisms alike — again treatment-independent.
5. LKB1-dependent immune defects exist but contribute too little ICI-specific harm to generate a detectable interaction.
6. Therefore STK11 HR > 1 on ICI, STK11 HR > 1 of similar size on chemotherapy, ratio of the two ≈ 1.

## 3. Primary endpoint

**OS — Overall survival**

- **Definition:** Days from index date to death from any cause; censored at last known contact.
- **Time origin:** index treatment start (regimen-level)
- **Censoring:** administrative at data cutoff; last contact otherwise
- **Time field:** `os_days` | **Event field:** `death_event`

## 4. Statistical analysis plan

- **Model:** Cox proportional hazards, single fit, no stepwise or automated selection
- **Covariates (fixed, complete list):**

  1. age at index
  2. sex
  3. smoking status
  4. histology
  5. tmb
  6. kras_mut
  7. keap1_mut
  8. tp53_mut

- **Alpha:** 0.05 (two-sided)
- **Multiple-testing correction:** Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at q<0.05 across the full pre-registered hypothesis set
- **Missing data:** complete-case for the covariate set above; the missingness count is reported alongside the estimate. No imputation.
- **Left truncation:** risk set entry at sequencing date where the exposure is genomic, to avoid immortal-time bias.
- **Proportional hazards:** Schoenfeld residual test is reported as a diagnostic. A PH violation is reported, not corrected by switching models — switching models after seeing the data is the p-hacking this structure exists to prevent.

**Measurement concerns:**

- The primary exposure is a variant call (genomic_variant, provenance 'genomic'), the strongest exposure ascertainment available here — not NLP. STK11 loss-of-function is constructible from alteration_type (nonsense / frameshift / splice / deletion) plus oncogenic_annotation for the missense arm, exactly as the hypothesis defines it.
- The population filter diagnosis.stage_at_dx is nlp_extracted, so the 'stage IIIB-IV or recurrent' inclusion carries unknown misclassification. by_stage gives Stage 4 = 3421 and Stage 1-3 = 4387 for NSCLC, but a recurrence after an early-stage diagnosis is advanced disease at the time of first-line systemic therapy and is not separable from the stage field alone; is_de_novo_metastatic (derived) and treatment timing partly corroborate.
- clinical_annotation_nlp.ecog_ps is nlp_extracted and nullable, and it is the covariate that most directly proxies the indication bias driving arm assignment. Its missingness is likely differential by arm — sicker patients on the non-ICI arm are also the ones whose notes are least complete — so the adjustment is weakest exactly where it is most needed.
- MNAR co-mutation via panel_version. The real distribution is IMPACT468 3919, IMPACT505 2228, IMPACT410 1336, IMPACT341 326. The hypothesis requires panels calling STK11, KRAS, KEAP1, TP53 and SMARCA4; the schema publishes no gene-by-panel coverage map, so the restriction must be executed against an external panel manifest attached to the cohort spec. If only IMPACT341 lacks a required gene the cost is 4.2%; if IMPACT410 also lacks one the cost is 21.3%, which would move every figure in the power block. Report the restricted denominator in the prereg before locking.
- Left truncation at days_dx_to_sequencing is mandatory. genomic_callable is 7476/7809, so 4.3% of NSCLC patients have no usable genomic ascertainment at all and are excluded, not imputed as wild-type.
- treatment.stop_day_offset must not be touched. Time-to-discontinuation is a locked outcome derivative; first-line regimen class is fully determined by drug_class + line_of_therapy + regimen_id + start_day_offset.

## 6. Novelty

- Literature-gap strength: 2.5
- Mechanistic plausibility: 5.0 (strong)
- Dataset feasibility: 2.5
- Composite: 3.375 (rank 1)

**Prior negative evidence considered:**

- `PMID 32312757 (Papillon-Cavanagh, ESMO Open 2020)` — Looked directly and found nothing: no interaction between STK11 mutation and anti-PD-1/PD-L1 treatment on real-world PFS (HR 1.05, 0.76-1.44, p=0.785) or OS (HR 1.13, 0.76-1.67, p=0.540). This is the strongest existing null and it substantially discounts novelty; the remaining gap is that it is a 2020 non-squamous-only cohort with 574 ICI-treated patients, underpowered for subgroup interaction and predating chemo-immunotherapy as the dominant first-line standard.
- `J Clin Oncol 43 (16_suppl) e20504, ASCO 2025 abstract` — Meta-analysis of six randomized and one retrospective comparative study: chemo-immunotherapy retained an OS benefit over chemotherapy alone despite STK11 mutation (HR 0.80, 0.67-0.97), KEAP1 (0.76, 0.59-0.98) and KRAS (0.67, 0.55-0.82). A randomized-comparator null for the predictive interpretation. Conference abstract only, not a full paper.
- `PMID 34500370` — Commentary/analysis explicitly titled 'STK11/LKB1 and KEAP1 mutations in non-small cell lung cancer: Prognostic rather than predictive?' — the prognostic interpretation is already in print, so the novelty here is the formal interaction estimate at modern scale, not the idea.
